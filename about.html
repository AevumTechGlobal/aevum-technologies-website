Our foundation

Who we are

A founder-led technology company. Focused on useful progress.

Aevum Technologies helps startups, businesses and individuals turn well-defined needs into thoughtful digital solutions. We bring a practical, security-aware perspective to technology decisions, from early exploration through implementation.

Our work is guided by curiosity, clear communication and respect for the people who use the systems we create. We aim to make technology easier to use, more dependable and relevant to the problem it is meant to solve.

01 / Vision

Build technology that matters.

We want to help businesses and individuals create smarter, more resilient digital experiences—and contribute to a world where technology is useful, accessible and thoughtfully applied.

02 / Mission

Make technology practical.

Our mission is to build valuable digital solutions, strengthen security and help people use technology with greater confidence, capability and opportunity.

Founder profile

The Founder Behind the Vision.

Emmanuel Godspower Nasarasoko

Founder, Aevum Technologies Ltd.

Emmanuel Godspower Nasarasoko is a software developer with a BSc in Computer Science from Federal University Lokoja. His interests include software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and practical digital innovation.

He is also recognised as NACOS Cyber Ace of the Year 2025, a mark of his growing focus on digital security and technology capability. At Aevum, that background shapes a practical, security-aware approach to building tools and helping people make better use of technology.
